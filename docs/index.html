How it works

Thirty seconds to the couch.

One small program on the PC. Nothing to sign up for, nothing in the cloud: it all stays on your Wi-Fi.

Run it on the PC

Start sofa-remote. It's a single file, about 5 MB, with the phone app built in.

Scan the QR code

Point the phone camera at the code it prints. The link carries its own access key.

Sit back

Play, pause, scroll, type, game. Add more PCs and switch between them from the top bar.

Features

Everything a remote should have been.

🖐️

A touchpad that feels local

Moves go out the moment your finger moves, over UDP, with smoothed acceleration. Tap to click, hold to right-click, tap-and-drag to select.

⏯️

Media & volume

Play, skip, seek and volume, from any app.

🎤

Type or talk

Live keyboard with voice typing, straight into the focused field.

📋

Shared clipboard

Send links and text both ways, or open a URL on the PC.

✏️

Your own layouts

Drag buttons, sticks and pads around on the phone. Macros included.

🖥️

Many PCs

One phone, every computer in the house. One button can hit them all.

🔊

Volume buttons

The Android app turns the phone's own buttons into PC controls, even when locked.

Gamepads

Every phone is a real controller.

Four friends, four phones, four players. Each phone shows up on the PC as its own Xbox controller, so couch co-op games, Steam and emulators just see gamepads.

  • Analog sticks, triggers, bumpers, d-pad, ABXY
  • Reconnect within 30 seconds and keep your player slot
  • Windows via ViGEmBus, Linux via uinput
  • Prefer keyboard and mouse? A WASD + mouse-look layout is built in too
